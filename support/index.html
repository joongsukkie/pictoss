Support

Something not working, or a photo grouped that shouldn't be? Open an issue and describe what happened. Please don't attach personal photos.

I deleted photos but my storage didn't go down

iOS keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days. To get the space back now, open Photos, scroll to Utilities → Recently Deleted, and choose Delete All. PicToss's "Open Photos" button takes you there after each clean-up.

Can I get a photo back?

Yes, for 30 days. Open Photos, go to Recently Deleted, select the photo and tap Recover.

It only found a few photos

If you chose "Limit Access" when PicToss asked for permission, it can only see the photos you picked. Go to Settings → Apps → PicToss → Photos and choose Full Access.

Some photos show "In iCloud"

With "Optimize iPhone Storage" turned on, some originals live only in iCloud. PicToss never downloads them, so it can't preview or measure those. That's on purpose: it never uses your data plan.

How does it decide which shot to keep?

In a group of similar shots it suggests the sharpest one, then the highest resolution, then the most recent. It only suggests. You decide every photo with a swipe.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no trial, no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.
