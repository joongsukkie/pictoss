JETTISON

Privacy policy

Effective 16 October 2026

The short version: Jettison does not collect, store, share or sell any of your data. It has no account system, no servers and no networking code.

What the app accesses

With your permission, Jettison reads the photo library on your device in order to find duplicate, similar, blurry and oversized items, and to delete the ones you choose. This happens entirely on your iPhone. Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted or copied anywhere.

Jettison only reads images already stored on the device. It does not download photos from iCloud to analyse them.

What the app stores

To make rescans fast, Jettison keeps a small cache of fingerprints on your device: short numeric summaries of each photo, used to tell whether two photos look alike. The cache cannot be turned back into your photos, never leaves the device, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Deleting photos

Jettison never deletes anything without your confirmation. Every deletion goes through the iOS system prompt, and deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where they can be restored for 30 days.

Data collection

None. Jettison contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Children

Jettison collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
